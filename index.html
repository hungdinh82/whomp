What is WHOMP?

WHOMP is a personal automation studio that helps a single creator produce original short-form videos (vertical 9:16, under 60 seconds) and publish them to the creator's own connected social media accounts, including TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook Reels.

Every video is generated from original scripts, synthetic voice-over, royalty-free background music, and licensed or stock visual assets. WHOMP only posts to accounts that the creator has personally authorized through official platform login (OAuth).

How it works

  • Generate — an original short script and matching voice-over are produced.
  • Compose — the script, captions, music, and stock visuals are rendered into a vertical video.
  • Review — the creator previews and approves each video before anything is published.
  • Publish — approved videos are uploaded to the creator's own connected accounts via official platform APIs.

Use of the TikTok Content Posting API

WHOMP uses the TikTok Content Posting API solely to upload videos to the authenticated creator's own TikTok account. WHOMP does not read, collect, or repost other users' content, and does not post without the account owner's explicit approval of each upload.
