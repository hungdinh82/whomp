Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WHOMP ("the Service") handles information in connection with publishing short-form videos to the operator's own connected social media accounts. WHOMP is operated by and for a single account owner and is not a multi-user public service.

1. Information We Access

  • Authentication tokens — When you connect a social account (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook) via official OAuth login, the Service stores the access and refresh tokens required to upload videos on your behalf.
  • Account identifiers — Basic identifiers (such as the connected account's open ID or page ID) needed to target uploads to the correct account.
  • Video content — The original videos and their metadata (title, description, tags) that you choose to publish.

2. How We Use Information

Accessed information is used solely to upload approved videos to your own connected accounts and to manage the scheduling of those uploads. WHOMP does not use this information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to publishing your own content.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not read, collect, or store other users' content or personal data.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not track end viewers of the published videos.

4. Data Storage and Security

Authentication tokens are stored locally on the operator's own device in a private environment file and are never transmitted to any third party other than the official platform APIs (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook) for the purpose of uploading content.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Tokens and identifiers are retained only as long as the corresponding account remains connected. You may revoke access at any time from the platform's own app-permissions settings (for example, TikTok → Settings → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions), which immediately invalidates WHOMP's access. Locally stored tokens can be deleted by removing them from the environment file.

6. Third-Party Platforms

WHOMP integrates with third-party platforms whose own privacy practices apply to data held on their systems:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Google / YouTube Privacy Policy
  • Meta / Facebook Privacy Policy

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

For any privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact hunggaksa@gmail.com.
